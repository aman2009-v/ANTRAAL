Our Story

The Vision

The ANTRAAL MUN was conceived with a singular vision: to create a platform where young minds can come together to discuss, debate, and decide on the most pressing issues of our time. We believe in empowering the next generation of leaders.

🌍

Global Perspective

With committees ranging from UNHRC to AIPPM and Lok Sabha, we offer diverse perspectives on international diplomacy. Our delegates experience the complexity and beauty of global governance firsthand.
